Football is the most popular sport in the world with billions of fans across every continent.
The FIFA World Cup attracts the largest global television audience of any sporting event.
Club football generates enormous revenue through broadcasting rights, sponsorships, and merchandise.

Basketball has grown into a truly global sport thanks to the influence of the NBA.
Players from Europe, Africa, and Asia now compete at the highest levels of professional basketball.
The three-point revolution has fundamentally changed how the game is played and coached.

Tennis Grand Slam tournaments including Wimbledon, the US Open, French Open, and Australian Open
attract the world's best players competing for prestigious titles and ranking points.
The rivalry between top players has elevated the sport's global popularity significantly.

Cricket dominates sports culture in countries like India, Australia, England, and Pakistan.
The Indian Premier League transformed cricket into a high-value entertainment franchise model.
Test cricket remains the purest and most technically demanding format of the game.

Olympic Games bring together athletes from over two hundred nations every four years.
The Games celebrate human achievement in sports ranging from athletics to swimming to gymnastics.
Host cities invest billions in infrastructure, hoping to boost tourism and international prestige.
