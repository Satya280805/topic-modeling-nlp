What is Topic Modeling?
Unsupervised NLP technique

Topic modeling is an unsupervised machine learning technique that automatically discovers hidden thematic structure in large collections of text. Unlike classification, you do not need labelled data — the algorithm finds patterns on its own.

It is used in news aggregation, academic research, customer feedback analysis, social media monitoring, legal document discovery, and much more.

📐
Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA)
Probabilistic generative model · Blei et al. 2003

LDA is a probabilistic generative model. It assumes documents are written by first picking a mixture of topics, then picking words from those topics. LDA reverses this process to infer topics from observed words.

Assumption 1: Each document is a mixture of K topics
Assumption 2: Each topic is a distribution over vocabulary
Goal: Given observed documents — infer topics + per-doc mixtures
Method: Variational Bayes EM algorithm (online variant, sklearn)

LDA uses Dirichlet priors (alpha and beta) to control how focused or spread-out topic mixtures and word distributions are. Lower alpha = sparser topic mixtures. Lower beta = more focused topics.

Processing Pipeline
Step-by-step flow inside nlp_engine.py
01
Text Extraction
Extract raw text from paste / .txt upload / .csv upload / URL scrape (BeautifulSoup)
02
Preprocessing
Lowercase, strip URLs/punctuation, remove stopwords, WordNetLemmatizer (NLTK)
03
Vectorisation
CountVectorizer builds Document-Term Matrix (Bag-of-Words model, top 1000 features)
04
LDA Training
LatentDirichletAllocation fits the DTM via online variational Bayes (scikit-learn)
05
Result Extraction
argsort() extracts top-N keywords + scores per topic. argmax() assigns each doc its dominant topic.

What Are Word Scores?
Reading the weight numbers

Every keyword on a topic card shows a small score next to it — e.g. climate 0.0412. This is the normalised word-topic probability from LDA's component matrix — how strongly that word defines the topic.

0.04+Very Strong signal
0.02-0.04Moderate signal
0.01-0.02Weak signal
below 0.01Minor / background

Computed as: word's raw LDA weight divided by total weights in that topic. Higher score = word appears more frequently and distinctively in documents belonging to that topic.

Interpreting Results
What to look for
High weight words — most representative of topic
Low perplexity — model fits data well
Coherent topics — words make semantic sense
Overlapping topics — normal for multithematic text
Increase K — for finer, narrower topics
Decrease K — for broader, general themes
More documents — better topic separation
